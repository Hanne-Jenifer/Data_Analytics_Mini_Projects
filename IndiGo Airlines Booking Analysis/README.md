# 🛩️ Indigo Airlines Booking Data Analysis & Dashboard

An Excel-based analysis of flight booking data focused on revenue, booking behaviour, customer characteristics, and service preferences.  
The project combines hypothesis-driven analysis with an interactive dashboard to identify patterns in booking and revenue performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Focus

The analysis investigates how booking characteristics and customer choices are associated with booking revenue, including:

1. Seat class and booking amount
2. Purchase lead and seat class
3. Length of stay and booking amount
4. Sales channel and booking amount
5. Flight day and booking trends
6. Additional services and booking amount

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **7,442 booking records** with variables covering:

- Booking and flight details
- Booking amount and passenger count
- Traveler type
- Sales channel
- Purchase lead
- Trip type
- Length and type of stay
- Flight day and flight time
- Booking origin and route
- Seat class
- Additional services
- Planned and actual flight duration

---

## 🔍 Data Understanding

The analysis examined booking behaviour across customer characteristics, travel patterns,
sales channels, seat classes, and optional services to identify relationships with revenue
and booking activity.

## 🧪 Hypothesis Analysis

### 1. Seat Class & Booking Amount
Compared booking revenue across different seat classes to examine whether seat class is
associated with differences in booking value.

### 2. Purchase Lead & Seat Class
Examined the relationship between how early a booking was made and the seat class selected.

### 3. Length of Stay & Booking Amount
Compared booking amounts across different lengths of stay to identify differences in
revenue by travel duration.

### 4. Sales Channel & Booking Amount
Compared booking performance across **Internet** and **Mobile** sales channels.

### 5. Flight Day & Booking Trends
Analysed booking activity across different days of the week to identify booking patterns.

### 6. Additional Services & Booking Amount
Examined whether selections such as **extra baggage, preferred seats, and in-flight meals**
are associated with differences in booking amount.

> **Note:** The project material currently available provides the hypotheses and dashboard
> structure, but not the underlying comparison values for these six analyses. No numerical
> findings are added here without supporting output.
