# 🛒 Flipkart Customer Service Analysis

This project analyzes **30,000 Flipkart customer service interactions** to evaluate customer
satisfaction and service performance across sentiment, CSAT, SLA compliance, call duration,
call reasons, regions, channels, and call centers using **Microsoft Excel**.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- **30K customer calls** were analyzed with an **average CSAT score of 5.54**.
- **SLA compliance was 62%**, meaning **38% of interactions were outside the SLA-compliant
  category**.
- **52% of interactions were classified as negative sentiment**, highlighting a substantial
  customer-experience concern within the analyzed calls.
- **Average call duration was 0.52 minutes**, providing a baseline for evaluating service
  interaction efficiency.

> **Note:** Detailed breakdowns by sentiment, call reason, region, channel, and call center are
> presented in the dashboard, but their numerical results are not documented in the available
> project material. No additional values are inferred here.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The analysis uses a Flipkart customer service call dataset containing **30,000 interactions**
across **13 columns**.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `id` | Unique identifier for the customer call |
| `customer_name` | Name of the customer |
| `Gender` | Customer gender |
| `sentiment` | Customer sentiment during the interaction |
| `csat_score` | Customer satisfaction score |
| `call_timestamp` | Date of the customer call |
| `Reasons` | Reason for contacting customer service |
| `City` | Customer city |
| `State` | Customer state |
| `Channel` | Customer service channel |
| `response_time` | Response-time SLA category |
| `call duration in minutes` | Duration of the customer call |
| `call_center` | Call center handling the interaction |

---

## 🔎 Analysis

The analysis examines customer service performance across:

- **Customer Sentiment** — Distribution of customer interactions by sentiment category.
- **Customer Satisfaction** — CSAT performance across customer interactions.
- **SLA Performance** — Response-time performance across SLA categories.
- **Call Duration** — Interaction duration as an operational efficiency measure.
- **Call Reasons** — Main reasons driving customer service contacts.
- **Regional Performance** — Customer service activity across locations.
- **Call Centers** — Comparison of activity across service centers.

## 💡 Recommendations

- Prioritize improvements in **SLA performance**, with **38% of interactions currently outside
  the SLA-compliant category**.
- Investigate the causes behind the **52% negative-sentiment rate** to identify opportunities
  for improving customer experience.
- Use the detailed dashboard breakdowns to identify specific **regions, call reasons, channels,
  and call centers** requiring further investigation.
