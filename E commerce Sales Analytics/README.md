# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis

This project analyzes **e-commerce sales data from 2023–2024** using **Power BI Desktop** to uncover
sales patterns across categories, months, and regions, with interactive filtering by **Category**
and **Payment Method**.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- **Total sales reached 339.43K** across **376 orders** and approximately **2K units sold**,
  with an **average order value of 902.73**.
- **Electronics generated 220K**, contributing approximately **65% of total sales** and making
  it the dominant product category.
- **Appliances generated 18K**, the lowest category sales, with Electronics generating more than
  **12×** its sales.
- **July recorded the highest monthly sales at 59K**, while **April was the lowest at 6K**,
  showing substantial variation in monthly performance.
- **West generated the highest regional sales at 80K**, followed by **North at 73K** and
  **Central at 71K**.
- **East recorded the lowest sales among the named regions at 53K**, creating a **27K gap**
  between the highest and lowest named regions.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains **376 sales records and 12 columns**, covering orders from
**January 2023 to December 2024**.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `OrderID` | Order identifier |
| `ProductID` | Product identifier |
| `ProductName` | Name of the product |
| `Category` | Product category |
| `Quantity` | Quantity sold |
| `UnitPrice` | Price per unit |
| `TotalPrice` | Total order price |
| `OrderDate` | Date of the order |
| `CustomerID` | Customer identifier |
| `CustomerName` | Customer name |
| `Region` | Sales region |
| `PaymentMethod` | Payment method used |

---

## 🔎 Analysis

The analysis evaluates e-commerce performance across three primary dimensions:

### 🛍️ Category Performance

Sales were compared across **Electronics, Furniture, Clothing, Books, and Appliances** to
identify the strongest and weakest contributors.

### 📅 Monthly Performance

Monthly sales were analyzed across the two-year period to identify periods of high and low
sales activity.

### 🌎 Regional Performance

Sales were compared across **West, North, Central, South, and East** to identify regional
differences and major contributors.
