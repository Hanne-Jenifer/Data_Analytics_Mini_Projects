# 💼 Naukri Job Market Analysis

This project analyzes **25,114 Naukri job postings** to identify job-market trends across roles, industries, experience requirements, salaries, applicants, and skills.  
The analysis focuses on data-related roles and uses Tableau to turn these patterns into an interactive dashboard for career-service decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze job-posting trends across industries and roles.
- Identify industries attracting higher applicant demand.
- Understand experience requirements across job positions.
- Analyze job-posting trends over time.
- Compare major data-related roles.
- Analyze available salary information.
- Measure demand for Tableau as a job skill.
- Build an interactive Tableau dashboard for career-service stakeholders.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains **25,114 job postings** and **16 columns**, including:

- Job Posting ID
- Job Posting Date
- Job Title
- Job Title Full
- Job Position Type
- Job Position Level
- Years of Experience
- Job Skills
- Job Location
- Minimum Pay
- Maximum Pay
- Pay Rate
- Number of Applicants
- Company Name
- Company Industry

**Time Period:** 2017–2021

### Data Quality

| Field | Missing Values |
|---|---:|
| Job Skills | 2,210 |
| Minimum Pay | 23,302 |
| Maximum Pay | 23,302 |

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 📈 Job Market Growth

Job postings increased consistently across the available years:

| Year | Job Postings |
|---|---:|
| 2017 | 3,001 |
| 2018 | 3,757 |
| 2019 | 3,877 |
| 2020 | 6,305 |
| 2021 | 8,174 |

The dataset shows a substantial increase in job-posting volume from **3,001 in 2017
to 8,174 in 2021**.

### 💻 Data-Related Roles

The selected data-related role group contains **9,261 postings** across:

- Data Engineer
- Business Analyst
- Data Scientist
- Data Analyst
- Machine Learning Engineer
- Data Science Manager

**Data Engineer** has the highest posting volume among these selected roles.

### 🏢 Industry Demand

For the selected data-related roles, the leading industries by job-posting volume are:

| Industry | Job Postings |
|---|---:|
| IT services | 1,097 |
| Software | 695 |
| Education | 624 |
| Streaming | 457 |
| Retail | 345 |

**IT services** leads the selected data-related roles in job-posting volume and
applicant demand.

### 📊 Experience Requirements

The selected data-related roles show the highest concentration of postings around
**3–5 years of experience**, with **4 years** representing the highest count within
this group.

### 💰 Salary Analysis

Salary analysis uses the available **Minimum Pay** and **Maximum Pay** fields to
derive average pay where both values are available.

The dataset also contains a **Pay Rate** field, so salary comparisons need to
consider the rate associated with each posting.

### 📌 Tableau Skill Demand

**2,331 job postings** mention Tableau in the `Job Skills` field, representing
approximately **9.28%** of all postings.

**661 postings** mention Tableau within the first three listed skills, representing
approximately **2.63%** of all postings.

## 💡 Business Takeaways

- Job-posting volume grew substantially between 2017 and 2021.
- IT services is the leading industry within the selected data-related roles.
- Data Engineer has the highest posting volume among the selected roles.
- Around 3–5 years of experience represents a major concentration of demand.
- Tableau appears as a listed skill in **2,331 postings**.
- Salary and applicant analyses require careful interpretation because of significant
  missing values.
- The dashboard can be used to compare roles, industries, experience levels, and
  skills for career guidance.
