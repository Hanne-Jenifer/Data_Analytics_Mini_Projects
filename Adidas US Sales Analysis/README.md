# Adidas US Sales Analysis & Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an analysis of Adidas sales performance across the United States using sales, product, retailer, regional, and sales-channel data.

The analysis focuses on identifying sales performance, profitability, product demand, regional contribution, retailer performance, and customer purchasing channels.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to transform the underlying sales data into meaningful business insights and performance indicators.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The primary objectives of this analysis are to:

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Evaluate total units sold
- Measure operating profit and operating margin
- Compare sales performance across regions
- Identify top-performing retailers
- Analyze product-level sales and unit performance
- Compare different sales methods
- Understand regional profitability
- Present key business metrics through an interactive dashboard

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of Adidas US sales performance.

### 1. Retailer Performance

Retailers are compared based on total operating profit to identify the strongest contributors to profitability.

The dashboard highlights retailers such as:

- West Gear
- Foot Locker
- Sports Direct
- Kohl's
- Amazon
- Walmart

---

### 2. Regional Sales Performance

Total sales are analyzed across the following regions:

- West
- Northeast
- Southeast
- South
- Midwest

This comparison helps identify regions contributing the highest overall sales.

---

### 3. Product Performance

Units sold are analyzed across major product categories, including:

- Men's Street Footwear
- Men's Athletic Footwear
- Women's Apparel
- Women's Street Footwear
- Women's Athletic Footwear
- Men's Apparel

This helps identify products with the strongest demand.

---

### 4. Regional Profitability

Operating profit is compared across regions to evaluate profitability in addition to sales performance.

This provides a better understanding of which regions are not only generating revenue but also contributing significantly to operating profit.

---

### 5. Sales Method Analysis

Sales are segmented by sales method:

- In-store
- Online
- Outlet

This allows comparison of the contribution of different sales channels to overall sales.

---

### 6. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

- Region
- Year

These filters allow users to interact with the dashboard and analyze performance for specific regions and periods.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis provides several business-level insights:

- Regional sales performance varies significantly across the US.
- The West region represents a major contributor to both sales and operating profit.
- Retailer performance differs considerably, with some retailers contributing substantially more profit than others.
- Men's Street Footwear is the strongest product category by units sold in the dashboard.
- Sales are distributed across in-store, online, and outlet channels, allowing channel-level performance comparison.
- Operating margin provides an additional profitability perspective beyond total sales.
