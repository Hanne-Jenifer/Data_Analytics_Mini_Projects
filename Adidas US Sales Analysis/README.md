# 👟 Adidas US Sales Analysis & Dashboard

## 📊 About

**Adidas US Sales Analysis** examines **2020–2021 Adidas sales data** across regions, retailers,
products, profitability, and sales methods, using **Microsoft Excel** to transform transactional
data into an interactive dashboard and business insights.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- **$899.90M** in total sales were generated from **2.48M units sold**, resulting in
  **$332.13M** in operating profit.
- The overall **average operating margin was 42.30%**, providing a profitability perspective
  beyond total revenue.
- The **West region** was one of the strongest contributors to overall sales and operating profit.
- **Men's Street Footwear** recorded the highest unit demand among the product categories analyzed.
- Retailer performance showed noticeable differences in operating profit contribution across
  the six retailers analyzed.
- Sales were distributed across **In-store, Online, and Outlet** methods, enabling comparison
  of channel-level performance.

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains Adidas US sales transactions covering **2020–2021**, with **13 fields**:

- Retailer
- Retailer ID
- Invoice Date
- Region
- State
- City
- Product
- Price per Unit
- Units Sold
- Total Sales
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin
- Sales Method

The data covers multiple US regions, retailers, product categories, and sales methods.

---

## 📈 Business Analysis

The analysis examines Adidas performance across four key areas:

### 🏪 Retailer Performance

Operating profit was compared across retailers to identify differences in profitability
contribution and the strongest-performing retailers.

### 🌎 Regional Performance

Sales and operating profit were compared across the **West, Northeast, Southeast, South,
and Midwest** regions to identify regional performance differences.

### 👟 Product Performance

Product categories were evaluated using units sold to identify demand patterns.
**Men's Street Footwear** emerged as the leading category by units sold.

### 🛍️ Sales Method

Sales were segmented across **In-store, Online, and Outlet** methods to compare their
contribution to overall sales.
